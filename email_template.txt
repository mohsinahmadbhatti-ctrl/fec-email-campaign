Most workplace challenges aren't skill gaps, they're awareness gaps. The bias a leader can't see, the toxicity tolerated a little too long, the difficult conversation that never happens.

Lead Forward is a two-day intensive built to address exactly that: awareness-led leadership for the people who shape how teams work.

11-12 August 2026 · PC Hotel, Karachi · In-person · Up to 25 participants

Day 1 — Managing Unconscious Bias at the Workplace
How bias quietly shapes hiring, promotions, feedback and everyday decisions, and how to interrupt it in real time and build fairer processes.
Trainer: Mohsin Ahmad Bhatti · Guest speaker: Sarfaraz Ahmed Rehman (Ex-CEO: Engro, FFC, PepsiCo)

Day 2 — Culture & Managing Workplace Toxicity
Diagnosing healthy vs. toxic team signals, the leader's role in psychological safety, handling cliques and disruptive behaviour, and resetting team norms.
Trainer: Khadija Hashimi · Guest speaker: Dustin Wood (Founder & Principal Consultant, Growth Edge)

Designed for senior leaders, heads of department, HR & people leaders, founders and business owners. Participants leave with a personal bias audit and action plan, frameworks for fair and inclusive decisions, techniques to address toxicity early, tools for courageous conversations, and a certificate of completion.

Seats are limited. Organisations sending a leadership cohort together tend to see the most lasting culture shift.
